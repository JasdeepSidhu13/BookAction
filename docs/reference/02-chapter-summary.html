Chapter 2

Decisions: The Pathway to Power

8 min read·5 key ideas·5 action questions
Summary Action Due
The big idea

Your life is shaped less by your circumstances than by your decisions. A real decision means committing to a result and cutting off every other option.

In this chapter

Key ideas The three decisions Key insight Apply it Watch out for

Key ideas

01

Decisions, not conditions, determine destiny

Two people in the same situation end up in very different places because of what they decide to do about it.

02

A true decision cuts off alternatives

"I'd like to" is a preference. A decision is a commitment with no back door.

03

Decide more often

Deciding is a muscle. The more decisions you make, the better you get at making them.

04

Stay committed to the goal, flexible on the approach

Hold the outcome steady and change tactics whenever something isn't working.

The three decisions you make every moment

What to focus on

Where you put your attention decides what you notice, and what you feel.

What it means

The meaning you give an event shapes how you respond to it.

What to do

The action you take turns focus and meaning into results.

Key insight

Not deciding is also a decision — one that hands your direction to circumstance.

Apply it

  • Write down two decisions you've been putting off.
  • For each one, take a first action within 24 hours.
  • Each evening, note one decision you made and what it taught you.

Watch out for

Waiting for certaintyYou will rarely have all the information. Decide, then adjust.
Keeping a back doorAn escape route turns a decision back into a wish.
